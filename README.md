# 龍之華語 · Chinese Quest 🐉

A **Montessori-inspired Traditional Chinese learning game** for primary-age
children at international schools. A four-strand language syllabus (聽 listen ·
說 speak · 讀 read · 寫 write), eight short stories under 100 characters, a
40-level adventure map, a daily four-skill check, a dragon companion, a
classroom leaderboard with a printable poster, a two-player battle mode and real
3D graphics.

- **Language:** Traditional Chinese, Taiwan (MOE) standard readings
- **Reading aids:** Hanyu pinyin (default) and/or Zhuyin 注音
- **Instructions:** bilingual (English + Chinese) and spoken aloud, so
  pre-readers can play unaided
- **No install, no accounts, no internet.** One folder you can copy to a USB
  stick, a class iPad or a school laptop.

---

## 1. Start playing

| How | Steps |
|---|---|
| **Easiest** | Double-click `index.html` — it opens in the browser and plays. Every font, picture, sound and 3D model is generated by the code itself, so it works offline. |
| **Most reliable for a class** | Serve the folder, then open the address on each device:<br>`cd chinese-quest && python3 -m http.server 8000`<br>then visit `http://localhost:8000` |

> **Why serve it?** Some browsers restrict `localStorage` on `file://` pages, in
> which case progress is kept only until the tab is closed. Served over
> `http://localhost`, every child's progress is saved permanently. Nothing ever
> leaves the device.

**First run:** the child types a name, picks a dragon, chooses a difficulty and a
reading aid. Any number of children can each keep their own dragon, XP, coins,
badges, levels, stories and word history on one device
(**Settings → Players → ➕ Add player**). The five tabs are
**🏠 主頁 Home · 🗺️ 關卡 Levels · 📖 讀本 Reader · 🏆 排行 Ranking · 🎒 收藏 Collection**,
with **Settings** behind the player chip.

**Turn on the Chinese voice:** *Settings → Test Chinese voice*. If the device has
no Chinese voice, the listening game falls back to showing pinyin instead.

## 2. The Montessori syllabus

The whole app is organised around **the four aspects of language**, in Maria
Montessori's order — spoken language first, then writing (encoding), then reading
(decoding):

| | Strand | What the child does | Montessori principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👂 | **聽 Listening** | Hears a word, finds the matching character | Sound games; the *second period* of a lesson — "show me…" |
| 🗣️ | **說 Speaking** | Says the word out loud, then checks it | Spoken language comes first; *first and third periods* — "this is… / what is this?" |
| 📖 | **讀 Reading** | Reads a word, then a real sentence from a story | Decoding follows encoding; only words the child has met |
| ✍️ | **寫 Writing** | Traces the character, then builds the word from characters | Sandpaper letters, then the *moveable alphabet* |

Design decisions taken straight from the method:

* **Control of error, not marking.** Tracing is scored against the character's
  own shape — the child sees their ink next to the model and corrects it. Nothing
  is timed.
* **Isolation of difficulty.** Every story is written from vocabulary the child
  has already met, so decoding is real reading, not guessing.
* **Concrete before abstract.** Picture and object first, character second,
  pinyin last (and pinyin can be switched off entirely for real reading).
* **A prepared environment.** Four strands, one visible path, one thing to do
  next — and free choice everywhere else.
* **Respect for the child's rhythm.** Three levels a day, then rest (§6). Replays
  of finished levels are always allowed, because repetition is mastery.

### 8 short stories — every one under 100 characters

The **📖 讀本 Reader** tab has a bookshelf of eight controlled-vocabulary
stories (32–46 characters each):

| Story | English | Level | Characters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 小貓 | The Kitten | 🌱 easy | 36 |
| 我的家人 | My Family | 🌱 easy | 43 |
| 小狗 | The Puppy | 🌱 easy | 32 |
| 好天氣 | A Beautiful Day | 🌿 medium | 46 |
| 早餐 | Breakfast | 🌿 medium | 42 |
| 在學校 | At School | 🌿 medium | 46 |
| 下雨天 | A Rainy Day | 🌳 challenge | 43 |
| 我的生日 | My Birthday | 🌳 challenge | 46 |

In the reader the child can:

* **tap any character** to hear it and see its reading — every character in the
  library is glossed (162 characters in all, including the sight words 我 是 的
  了 在 有… that a reader needs long before they can name them)
* switch **pinyin on/off** (shown as ruby text above each character), and
  **English on/off**
* hear the **whole story read aloud**
* answer **comprehension questions** and win stars for it

Every story is validated automatically: under 100 characters, every character
carries a reading, every question has a fair single answer.

## 3. The daily four-skill check 🎯

A short, friendly check of all four strands — **the daily game**:

* **12 items: 3 × 聽, 3 × 說, 3 × 讀, 3 × 寫** (two characters traced plus one word
  built with the moveable alphabet). No timer, no pressure.
* Ends with a **report card** showing a bar and a score for each strand, a total,
  a grade out of three stars — and it is **kept for the day**, so you can look at
  it again by tapping the card.
* Speaking and writing are **self- or grown-up assessed** (a spoken answer can be
  scored by speech recognition where the browser supports it, or by the child or
  a parent listening back) — the app says so on the card.
* One scored check a day, unlimited practice. Full marks in all four strands
  earns the 🌟 badge.

## 4. The adventure map 🗺️ (40 levels)

**40 levels = 10 themes × 3 skill levels + 1 boss**, on a winding road you scroll
down like a level map:

* Each theme contributes three levels that get longer as they climb — 6, then 8,
  then 10 questions — in a different mode (picture → listening → pinyin), and
  ends with a **boss level** 👑 (a Dragon Battle duel or a Boss Rush).
* **The road you have walked is lit gold**, the road ahead is dimmed; each badge
  shows its number, its game mode and **the stars you won there** (★★☆).
* **Your dragon sits on the level you are up to** with a bouncing marker, and the
  map opens scrolled to that spot. A sticky header keeps the progress bar, the
  star total (⭐ 28 / 120) and a ▶ 第 N 關 button on screen.
* **One star unlocks the next level.** A first clear pays 🪙10 (+10 more the very
  first time), three stars pay 🪙20. Boss difficulty scales with progress; losing
  a duel still earns a star if you got the rival below 30 HP.

## 5. The eight game modes

| Mode | Chinese | What it trains |
|---|---|---|
| 🎯 **Daily Review** | 每日複習 | The adaptive mixed round — the words this child needs most today |
| 🃏 **Memory Match** | 配對樂 | Character ↔ picture recognition, concentration |
| 👂 **Listen Up** | 聽力王 | Listening discrimination → choosing the right characters |
| 🔤 **Pinyin Pop** | 拼音高手 | Reading pinyin (or zhuyin) and matching it to a character |
| 🖼️ **Picture Pick** | 看圖選字 | Meaning → character recall |
| 🧩 **Word Builder** | 拼字高手 | Character order in multi-character words |
| 🚀 **Boss Rush** | 挑戰王 | Speed, mixed skills, 3 lives, 60 seconds, combos |
| ⚔️ **Dragon Battle** | 龍之對戰 | Everything above, under pressure, against a rival |

Every round ends in a result card with stars, XP, coins and the exact words
practised. **Keyboard:** `1`–`6` answer, `Space` replays the audio, `Backspace`
takes back a tile in Word Builder.

---

## 6. Daily rhythm: three levels a day 🌙

To protect the child's rhythm (and to keep the game worth coming back to), only
**3 new levels can be passed each day**:

* Clearing a level spends one of the day's three; **replaying a level you have
  already passed never spends anything** — repetition is how mastery happens.
* When the day's levels are gone, the next level shows a 🌙 and explains itself:
  *"今天已經闖了 3 關！明天再來，先做四項檢查或讀故事吧."* The map header counts
  down: **🌙 今日剩 2 關**.
* Teachers can change it in **Settings → 🌙 每天最多闖幾關**: 1, 3, 5 or no limit.

## 7. Dragon Battle ⚔️ (one or two children)

> **The first player to answer correctly wins the exchange and damages the rival
> dragon. A wrong answer freezes you until the next question.**

* **🤖 Solo — Battle the Dragon King.** Three rivals (🦎 Hatchling, 🐲 Fire
  Dragon, 🐉 Dragon King) with their own accuracy and reaction time, introduced
  by a spinning **3D coin toss**.
* **👥 Two-player duel.** Two children, one device, split screen: Player 1 (left)
  on **A S D F**, Player 2 (right) on **J K L ;** — or each taps their own half.
* 100 HP each, 14–28 damage per won exchange plus a combo bonus of up to +12.
* Player 2 can be a guest or another saved player — in which case *both*
  children's word histories, XP and coins are updated.

## 8. Ranking and the poster 🏆

* **🏅 All-time board** — every player on this device, ranked by XP, with their
  dragon, mastered words, stars, streak and badges.
* **📅 Weekly league** — ranked by *this week's* XP only, resetting every Monday,
  so a new child can win something immediately.
* **🖼️ Make poster** — renders a 1080×1350 poster (3D trophy and podium, top
  eight, the date) to **download as PNG** or **print on A4** for the classroom
  wall. The ranking is device-local by design: no accounts, nothing uploaded.

## 9. The 3D graphics 🧊

Real 3D, rendered in software — no WebGL, no model files, no downloads:

* **A hand-written renderer** (`js/engine3d.js`): procedural meshes (surface of
  revolution), perspective projection, painter's-algorithm depth sorting and flat
  Lambert shading, drawn through the plain canvas 2D API.
* **Where you see it:** a rotating **power orb** behind the pet that grows from a
  glowing egg into a faceted gem and a spiked golden star as the dragon evolves;
  the **gold coin** that starts every duel; the **trophy** above the ranking
  podium and on the poster; CSS-3D perspective on the adventure map, the podium
  and card hovers.
* **Switchable:** *Settings → 🧊 3D graphics* pauses every 3D canvas instantly.
  `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected too.

## 10. For teachers and parents

* **Difficulty** (*Settings*): **Easy** — 3 options, no timer, 4 memory pairs.
  **Normal** — 4 options, 12 s per question, 6 pairs. **Hard** — 6 options, 8 s
  per question, 8 pairs.
* **Reading aid** (*Settings*): pinyin / zhuyin / both / neither. Turn both off to
  test pure character recognition.
* **Progress report** (*Settings*): games played, total answers, accuracy, best
  combo, words practised out of 109, words mastered, streak, duel record.
* **Vocabulary:** 10 themes — 數字 Numbers (11), 顏色 Colors (10), 動物 Animals
  (12), 食物 Food (12), 大自然 Nature (11), 身體 Body (10), 學校 School (11),
  動作 Actions (12), 家人 Family (10), 問候 Greetings (10) = **109 words**, plus
  162 characters readable in the story library.
* **Reset** clears one child's progress only, after a confirmation prompt.

## 11. Changing the content

**Vocabulary** lives in `js/data.js`:

```js
{ zh: '貓', py: 'māo', zy: 'ㄇㄠ', en: 'cat', em: '🐱', mn: 'The animal radical 犭 + 苗. A cat says māo!' }
```

`zh` traditional characters · `py` pinyin with tone marks, one syllable per
character, space separated · `zy` zhuyin, `˙` before a neutral-tone syllable ·
`em` one emoji picture · `mn` optional memory hook.

Adding a theme adds its 4 levels, flashcards and quiz pool automatically.

**Stories** live in `js/stories.js` — add an object to `STORIES` with `lines`
(Chinese + English pairs) and `questions`, and add any new character to
`STORY_WORDS` so the child can tap it for its reading. Then run the validators.

> **One CSS rule worth knowing.** An element that needs a width, height,
> transform or animation must be a **block-level box** (or a flex/grid item).
> Plain inline elements — `<span>`, `<i>`, `<a>` — silently ignore all of them.
> `node tools/check-css.js` fails the build if any markup violates this, because
> it is invisible in the source and only shows up as a blank area on screen.
>
> **And one DOM rule:** never give two elements the same `id`. An
> element-scoped `#id` lookup resolves document-wide, so the second host
> resolves to the first host's stale node. The four-skill engine uses classes
> for every internal handle for exactly this reason.

`test-dom.js` boots `index.html` in a real DOM (using the jsdom bundled with the
DSH desktop app) and plays all eight game modes, both battle modes, a level and
its unlock, the **daily level limit**, all **four skill strands**, the **daily
check** and its report card, the **story reader** with its comprehension
questions, the ranking poster, the shop, the settings and a reset — verifying
that a wrong battle answer freezes you, that passing a level unlocks the next,
that the fourth level of the day is refused, and that progress survives a reload.
It loads the scripts **from `index.html` itself**, so the test and the page can
never drift apart.

## 13. Notes

* **Privacy:** no network requests, no analytics, no accounts. All progress is in
  the browser's `localStorage` under the key `chineseQuest.v2`.
* **Accessibility:** minimum 54 px tap targets, high contrast,
  `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, and every instruction can be read aloud in
  English and Chinese with the 🔊 button in any game.
* **Works on:** Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox — phones, tablets, laptops and
  interactive whiteboards. Speech recognition, when available, upgrades the
  speaking strand from self-assessment to real scoring; without it the child
  records and compares, which is the Montessori control of error anyway.
